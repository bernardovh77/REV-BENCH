## Stage 071 — Procedural bottom end and manual kinematics

**Dependencies:** [037](../../docs/roadmap/03-contracts-and-sources.md#stage-037), [070](../../docs/roadmap/08-3d.md#stage-070)

**Deliverable:** Generate inline/V/boxer block and bores, pistons, wrist pins, rods, crank pins/counterweights and flywheel from the canonical dimensions. Add hand crank, cutaway/x-ray and geometry rebuilds.

**Required exit evidence:** Numerical transforms match core slider-crank for all five presets at TDC/BDC and off-axis angles; correct bank/pin/firing orientation, dimensions and bounds. Bore/stroke/layout edits resize geometry; screenshots plus automated transform checks retained.

## Stage 073 — Coherent snapshots and sound-synchronized live animation

**Dependencies:** [037](../../docs/roadmap/03-contracts-and-sources.md#stage-037), [066](../../docs/roadmap/07-browser.md#stage-066), [072](../../docs/roadmap/08-3d.md#stage-072)

**Deliverable:** Transport versioned timestamped snapshots with unwrapped cycle count, phase, RPM, events and cylinder/valve state. Align interpolated presentation with measured DSP/filter, buffering and device latency and handle sequence gaps, rebuild generations and pause/resume. Name each synchronization reference event and retain its physical gas-path/listener flight delay; graphics alignment must not cancel real propagation.

**Required exit evidence:** Instrument deterministic audio-event/display-time comparisons at acceleration, 6000 rpm and every supported limit. Measure modeled phase/time error separately from frame-sampling aliasing; freeze achievable budgets, not an unsupported few-degree promise.

**Stop/reject rule:** Wrapped-angle shortest-arc interpolation can become ambiguous even before one revolution per frame; require unwrapped phase/time at every speed. No stale geometry/state pairing or fresh audio paired with delayed unrelated animation.

**Legacy coverage:** 18 (live motion).

**Starting evidence/specification:** [GLOSSARY.md](../../GLOSSARY.md).

## Stage 075 — Shared 3D intake and complete exhaust routing

**Dependencies:** [044](../../docs/roadmap/04-propagation.md#stage-044), [049](../../docs/roadmap/04-propagation.md#stage-049), [071](../../docs/roadmap/08-3d.md#stage-071), [073](../../docs/roadmap/08-3d.md#stage-073)

**Deliverable:** Build intake, headers, collectors, crossovers, catalysts, mufflers and outlets from shared semantic geometry and centerlines. Connect source/listener frames and engine edits to the same description.

## Stage 076 — Gas, combustion, pulse and thermal visualization

**Dependencies:** [047](../../docs/roadmap/04-propagation.md#stage-047), [074](../../docs/roadmap/08-3d.md#stage-074), [075](../../docs/roadmap/08-3d.md#stage-075)

**Deliverable:** Visualize resolved pressure, gas temperature, composition, valve flow and pipe-cell pulses with legends and inspection. Burn fraction may drive an explicitly illustrative combustion indication; metal glow needs wall state or an explicit illustrative label.

**Required exit evidence:** Displayed values match snapshot fields at known states; color scales/units/ranges are testable and color-independent alternatives exist. Live versus slowed diagnostics handle sub-frame pulse travel truthfully; physical versus illustrative effects are documented.

**Stop/reject rule:** 0D burn fraction is not a spatial flame front; 1D flow is not CFD pathlines; gas temperature is not metal temperature. No unearned CFD or structural accuracy claims.

**Legacy coverage:** 19 (state visualization).

**Starting evidence/specification:** [docs/stage-S2d-design.md](../../docs/stage-S2d-design.md), [GLOSSARY.md](../../GLOSSARY.md).
