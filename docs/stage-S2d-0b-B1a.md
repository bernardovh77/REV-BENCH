## Implemented scope

**S2d-R:** a declared checkpoint inventory and one standard-library publisher
rebuild the complete result. `renders/checkpoint/manifest.json` registers
hashed self-contained research/reference sections and all retained diagnostic
assets. The standard 34 examples coexist with 42 declared experiment WAVs;
unknown extra files still fail validation. Replacing a render carries those
declared diagnostic assets before validating/installing the new checkpoint.
Each research/reference producer registers its section through the same
composition function. A normal render no longer discards those sections.

The first migration uses `--adopt-current` once; subsequent republication is
`python3 -S tools/render_results.py --publish-only`. Fresh render, repeated
publication, changed/missing input and failed-install behavior are exercised
through the actual publisher/replacement interfaces. No numerical packages are
required for republication. Reference/measurement files are never normalized
or regenerated by it. The accepted 48 audio payloads remain the baseline.

**S2d-0b:** B0 exposes `rb_ac_receive`, `rb_ac_incident` and `rb_ac_commit`.
Receive predicts the current arriving waves without consuming delay/all-pass
storage or work. The caller can solve its boundary before commit. Invalid
drives can be corrected and retried; duplicate receive or commit in the wrong
phase is rejected. The old `rb_ac_step` remains a wrapper and produces
bit-identical pressure/storage results in the tested fractional-delay control.
At a source, flow into the graph is the negative of the existing one-port
node-flow helper. Plans remain immutable after preparation.

**S2d-B1a:** `experiments/acoustics/coupled_duct.[ch]` implements an **open**
uniform-duct proof. Gas actually crosses the interface; an impermeable
diaphragm is not substituted for exhaust transport. It retains one conserved
state per cell: density, momentum, total energy and a passive tagged-species
density. The complementary species is total density minus tagged density.
No independent duplicate acoustic gas state or acoustic-energy reservoir is
advanced alongside that physical state.

This is a new bounded coupled-duct implementation, **not** an adapter from a
full production engine to the arbitrary B0 multiport graph. The B0 phased
interface and this open-duct proof establish complementary prerequisites.
Production mixture EOS, valves, complete engine/exhaust geometry compilation,
all production cap traces, packing/perforates and multiport mean-flow coupling
remain parent-stage work.

## Method and physical ownership

The nonlinear conservative Euler formulation and its small-disturbance
characteristics follow the [Clawpack Euler reference](https://www.clawpack.org/riemann_book/html/Euler_approximate.html)
and [acoustics reference](https://www.clawpack.org/riemann_book/html/Acoustics.html).
The particular conservative residual split and interface experiment below are
our implementation, tested against an independent simple-wave solution; the
references do not certify this code or its real-exhaust validity.

For the initially homogeneous zero-velocity reference, write the physical flux
as `F(U) = F_linear(U) + R(U)`. With momentum density `m`, gamma `g`, reference
enthalpy `h0` and reference tagged fraction `Y0`:

```text
U = [rho, m, E, tagged_mass_density]
pi = (g-1)*(E-E0)
a = (pi+c0*m)/2; b = (pi-c0*m)/2
zeta = rho-rho0-pi/c0^2
eta = tagged_mass_density-Y0*rho

F_linear = [m, p0+pi, h0*m, Y0*m]
R = [0,
     (3-g)*m^2/(2*rho),
     m*(g*E/rho-h0)-(g-1)*m^3/(2*rho^2),
     (tagged_mass_density/rho-Y0)*m]
```

The downstream line streams `a` and `b` through sample-aligned delays.
Stationary linear contact/entropy variables remain explicit. Spatial,
conservative nonlinear-residual RK2 half-steps transport their nonlinear
corrections; Rusanov stabilization is numerical viscosity, not physical
absorption. These corrections retain a downstream grid at the common clock.
There is **no established runtime saving from a cheap reduced mean model**.

The upstream retained gas region uses MC reconstruction/RK2, with two internal
half-tick linear substeps. Its four stage contributions produce one accepted
outgoing characteristic, with weights one quarter each. The current returning
characteristic is supplied before that solve. The same integrated conservative
flux is used with opposite signs in the two regions; the shared nonlinear
face flux is also counted once. Neither pressure overwriting after integration
nor an extra prescribed combustion pulse is used.

The common coupling tick is 192 kHz; internal RK substeps are explicit in the
method, not a separate low-rate telemetry/audio driver. `dx=c0/rate` is required
in this bounded proof. Unsupported non-sample-aligned lengths are rejected,
not rounded. The actual gas pressure is
`p=(g-1)*(E-m^2/(2*rho))`; `a+b` is a linearized conserved-state characteristic
amplitude, **not** physical pressure perturbation at finite velocity. Actual
volume flow likewise differs from its reference-density characteristic value.
Transferred energy is already total energy/enthalpy work; do not add `p'Q`
again. State/CFL failures are explicit and invalidate the candidate; there are
no hidden repair clamps. Derived prepared-state overflow is rejected too.

## Experiment and acceptance

The measured pipe has 560 cells, length 1.0004167 m, area 0.003 m², reference
sound speed 343 m/s, density 1.2 kg/m³ and gamma 1.4. The nonlinear/characteristic
interface is placed at cells 168 and 280 of the **same physical pipe**; 560 is
an all-FV control of this split method, not a relabeling of the older HLLC A1.

- Nine pre-shock simple-wave cases: relative initial pressure amplitudes
  `1e-5 / .001 / .01`, both interface locations and the all-FV control.
  The independent reference solves the full Euler right-simple-wave
  characteristic relation before shock formation. The test screen is 2%
  waveform L2 error for the two coupled locations; this is not a new verified
  harmonic bandwidth or a universal acoustics standard.
- Three termination controls at amplitude .001: ideal reflection `-1/0/+1`.
  Upstream gas pressure must respond with the expected return polarity and
  round-trip timing. The screen allows five common ticks of discretization
  error; matched return must be below .1 Pa in the stated return window.
- Three **one-second** source-free weak-pulse screens use the same terminations
  and a matched left boundary. Perturbation energy is sampled every 32 ticks;
  maximum ≤1.02 times initial and final <.001 times initial are declared
  engineering screens. These are leaky-domain prolonged tests, **not** a
  trapped closed-loop proof. B0 retains its separate one-second closed loops.
- Both regions independently book accepted mass, momentum, total energy and
  tagged mass. Residuals use quantity-specific reference inventories and a
  relative `1e-10` screen. The zero-size right region in the all-FV control
  must have zero transfer. Raw dimensional residuals are retained as well.
- Native tests check a nonuniform stationary entropy/tag contact and moving
  tagged contacts at ±6.86 m/s before exterior disturbances arrive. Integrated
  tagged transfer is compared with analytic advection; uniform composition
  alone would not exercise this transport. They also distinguish actual
  pressure from linearized characteristic amplitudes at finite velocity.
- Three interleaved timing repeats at each interface position exclude trace
  recording and file I/O. They time the whole fixture, including the retained
  correction grid; no engine/WASM/callback speedup is inferred.

An initial absolute `1e-9` screen combined kg, N·s and J into one number and
rejected the one-second double-precision energy book. That failure is retained
as `rejected-absolute-balance-screen.json`. The energy discrepancy was about
`2.5e-9 J` against hundreds of joules of background inventory; a separate
80-bit `long double` control reduced its matched-case right-region residual
to about `1.0e-12 J`. The control identifies accumulated floating-point
roundoff rather than evidence of acoustic growth. Quantity-specific relative
books and independently measured perturbation-energy growth remain separate
checks; neither is substituted for the other.

Final source hashes, all case commands, physical fields, time traces, per-region
books, source-free envelopes and costs are retained under ignored
`renders/evidence/coupling-proof-2026-10-23/`. The source revision **and working
tree status** are recorded, avoiding the earlier ambiguity between a HEAD
commit and the actual measured files. No MP3/filtered reference is a numerical
acceptance target here; those remain listening context, with raw float inputs
reserved for later engine comparisons.
