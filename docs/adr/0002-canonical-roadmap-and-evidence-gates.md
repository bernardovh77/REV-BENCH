# One canonical roadmap with evidence-gated promotion

On 2026-10-23 Bernardo requested a complete, renumbered plan and confirmed the
full original first-release scope and the order “shared contracts, sound gates,
browser, then 3D.” PLAN.md now owns one stable stage sequence and status table;
phase catalogs define bounded work, while a lossless crosswalk preserves old
IDs, evidence and unfinished obligations. This replaces overlapping parent,
sound-track and suffix numbers without rewriting their historical conclusions.

Prototype investigation, production integration, actual browser/runtime
qualification and Bernardo's realism/appearance acceptance are separate gates.
Learned synthesis and individually enumerated extras are conditional branches,
so speculative research cannot silently become a release prerequisite. A failed
candidate remains a documented result and does not force a production switch.
Future stages require predeclared independent evidence, not promises of zero
rework or claims that their tests have already run. Claude's setup remains active.
